FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about GlucoSense and its diabetes risk awareness tools.

What is GlucoSense?

GlucoSense is a web-based diabetes risk awareness platform that uses machine learning to provide an estimated risk result from selected health parameters.

Is the prediction a medical diagnosis?

No. The prediction is for awareness only and should not replace advice, diagnosis, or treatment from a qualified healthcare professional.

Do I need an account for a quick prediction?

No. The quick prediction tool can be used without logging in. An account is useful for saving and reviewing your reports.

What information is used for the prediction?

The assessment uses the health parameters requested by the prediction form, such as glucose, blood pressure, BMI, age, and related inputs.

Can I view previous predictions?

Yes. Registered users can access their saved prediction history through the patient dashboard.

What should I do if the result shows higher risk?

Use the result as an awareness signal and consult a qualified healthcare professional for proper evaluation and medical guidance.
